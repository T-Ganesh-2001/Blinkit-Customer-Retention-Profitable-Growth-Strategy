# Blinkit: Customer Retention & Profitable Growth Strategy

## 📌 About the Project

This project focuses on understanding customer behaviour in the quick-commerce industry and exploring how a platform like Blinkit could improve customer retention, encourage repeat purchases, and achieve more sustainable growth without relying excessively on promotional discounts.

The project will follow a practical, end-to-end product analytics and strategy approach. Starting with business problem identification, I will analyse customer and order data, identify behavioural patterns, investigate potential retention challenges, and develop data-driven product improvement recommendations.

Beyond data analysis, the project will involve building an interactive dashboard, developing a working product prototype, designing an A/B testing framework, and estimating the potential business impact of the proposed solution.

The objective is to connect **data analysis with real product decisions** and demonstrate how analytical insights can inform product strategy.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Understand customer purchasing behaviour and repeat-order patterns.
* Analyse customer retention and identify potential reasons customers may not return.
* Investigate the relationship between promotional discounts and repeat purchasing.
* Identify meaningful customer segments and potential growth opportunities.
* Use data to uncover actionable insights and support product decisions.
* Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate findings.
* Identify and prioritise product improvements based on customer needs and business value.
* Build a working prototype of a potential product solution.
* Design an A/B testing framework to evaluate the proposed improvement.
* Estimate potential business impact using transparent assumptions.
* Develop a final product strategy and implementation roadmap.

---

## ❓ Key Business Question

**How can Blinkit improve customer retention and repeat purchase frequency while reducing unnecessary dependence on promotional discounts?**

To investigate this question, I will analyse customer behaviour, purchasing patterns, discount usage, and other relevant factors available in the dataset.

Rather than assuming the cause of a retention problem or selecting a solution in advance, I will use the findings to identify potential opportunities and recommend an appropriate product improvement.

## 🔍 Project Workflow

### Phase 1: Business Understanding & Problem Definition

Understand the quick-commerce business model, define the customer problem, establish project objectives, and identify hypotheses that can be investigated through data.

### Phase 2: Dataset Preparation & Data Cleaning

Prepare suitable datasets, understand their structure, identify data-quality issues, handle missing values and duplicates where appropriate, and document the data dictionary and assumptions.

### Phase 3: Exploratory Data Analysis

Explore customer behaviour, order patterns, purchasing frequency, discount usage, and other relevant variables to identify meaningful patterns and questions for further investigation.

### Phase 4: SQL-Based Product Analytics

Use SQL to analyse customer segments, repeat purchases, retention patterns, order frequency, and discount-related behaviour. Develop queries that support reproducible analysis and measurable business insights.

### Phase 5: Customer Segmentation & Retention Analysis

Compare customer groups, investigate differences in purchasing behaviour, and identify potential retention opportunities based on the available evidence.

### Phase 6: Power BI Dashboard Development

Build an interactive dashboard featuring relevant KPIs, customer behaviour analysis, retention patterns, and discount-related insights. Each visual will communicate a meaningful finding or support a business decision.

### Phase 7: Product Opportunity Identification

Translate analytical findings into potential product improvements. Compare alternatives and prioritise opportunities based on customer impact, business value, implementation effort, and confidence in the evidence.

### Phase 8: Product Prototype Development

Build a working demonstration of the selected product improvement using Python and Streamlit, where appropriate. The prototype will illustrate how the proposed solution could work and how it addresses the identified customer problem.

### Phase 9: A/B Testing & Experimentation

Design an experiment to compare the proposed improvement against a baseline experience. Define the hypothesis, control and treatment groups, primary success metric, guardrail metrics, and evaluation criteria. If real experimentation is unavailable, use a clearly labelled simulation to demonstrate the methodology.

### Phase 10: Business Impact Analysis

Estimate the potential impact of the proposed solution by considering repeat orders, order value, promotional spending, estimated contribution, and relevant costs. Evaluate alternative scenarios using documented assumptions.

### Phase 11: Final Product Strategy & Recommendations

Consolidate the analysis, dashboard insights, product recommendation, prototype, experiment design, and business impact model into a final case study with a prioritised implementation roadmap.

## 📊 Key Metrics to Investigate

Depending on the available data, the analysis may include:

* Customer retention rate
* Repeat purchase rate
* Orders per customer
* Average order value (AOV)
* Customer purchase frequency
* Discount rate
* Customer conversion rate
* Estimated contribution per order

Metric definitions and observation periods will be established before drawing conclusions. The final selection will depend on the analytical questions and available data.

## ⚠️ Data Transparency

This is an independent educational portfolio project. Synthetic datasets may be generated to demonstrate analytical methods and product decision-making.

Synthetic data, simulated experiments, assumptions, and estimated business outcomes will be clearly identified. They will not be presented as actual Blinkit internal data, real-world experimental results, or verified company performance.

The project aims to demonstrate a practical analytical process rather than claim access to proprietary company information.

## 🚀 Project Goal

The ultimate goal is to demonstrate how a Product Analyst can move beyond reporting metrics to investigate customer problems, uncover meaningful insights, identify product opportunities, evaluate solutions, and connect product decisions with measurable business outcomes.

**The complete journey:**

**Business Problem → Data Analysis → Customer Insights → Product Strategy → Working Prototype → Experimentation → Business Impact**

This project is intended to demonstrate practical skills in product analytics, SQL, data visualisation, experimentation, and evidence-based product strategy through an end-to-end implementation.
